Stress Analyser

  • 10 questions on stress, sleep, exercise, diet, breaks, social support, relaxation, workload, enjoyment and motivation.
  • Each answer counts +1, 0 or −1, which gives a net score from −10 to +10 and a wellbeing percentage.
  • Four result bands, from “very good: low stress” to “high stress: take action”, each with its own suggestions.
  • It is a self-reflection tool, not a medical one.

Zen Memory Match

A short, calming break: 16 cards with 8 pairs of nature emoji, shuffled every game, with a live count of the pairs you have found.

Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks and nothing to install. Nothing you answer leaves your device.

Result

Shortlisted at Smart India Hackathon 2025. It was my first hackathon.
